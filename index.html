Questions people ask before they call.

Most full backyard redesigns in San Diego and Orange County land between $18,000 and $65,000 depending on square footage, hardscape, and whether irrigation or lighting is included. Smaller planting-only refreshes start around $4,500. Every quote is itemized before you sign anything.

Design and proposal usually take two to three weeks. Once you approve it, build time runs four to ten weeks depending on scope, permitting, and material lead times. You'll get a written schedule before crews break ground, and weekly updates while they're on site.

Yes. We pull city permits for grading, retaining walls, and electrical work, and we prepare the drawings most HOA architectural committees ask for. If your community requires a formal submission, we handle the paperwork and attend the review if needed.

Either. During the site walk we'll flag mature trees, healthy shrubs, and hardscape worth keeping, and design around them. Most projects are a mix of retained and new. A full teardown only happens if the site genuinely calls for it.

New plantings get a 90-day establishment visit schedule at no extra cost. After that, most clients move to a seasonal maintenance plan, biweekly or monthly, covering pruning, irrigation checks, and seasonal color. It's optional, not required.

We work with two third-party lenders who offer fixed-rate project financing, typically 6 to 60 month terms. We'll include financing estimates alongside the cash price in your proposal so you can compare both.
